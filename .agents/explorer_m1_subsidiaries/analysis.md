Arc Zist Azma houses specialized, climate-controlled laboratory suites equipped with state-of-the-art analytical instrumentation compliant with WHO-GMP and ISO/IEC 17025 principles:

- **Purity & Charge Heterogeneity:** Reverse-Phase HPLC (RP-HPLC), Hydrophobic Interaction Chromatography (HIC), and Capillary Electrophoresis (CE-SDS, capillary Isoelectric Focusing - cIEF).
- **Compendial Physicochemical Tests:** Osmolality, pH, visible/sub-visible particulate matter (light obscuration), and total protein concentration.

#### 4. میکروبیولوژی و کنترل محیطی (Microbiology & Environmental Safety Division)
- **Sterility Testing (آزمون استریلیتی):** Membrane filtration and direct inoculation in Class A isolators.
- **Bacterial Endotoxins Testing (BET / آزمون اندوتوکسین باکتریایی):** Kinetic chromogenic and turbidimetric LAL (Limulus Amebocyte Lysate) assays.
- **Bioburden Determination & Microbial Limits:** Raw material and in-process microbial enumeration.
- **Cleanroom Environmental Monitoring:** Air, surface, and particulate monitoring support for biopharma manufacturing facilities.

#### 5. مطالعات پایداری (ICH Stability Studies)
- **Real-Time & Accelerated Stability Testing:** Programmed climate chambers (ICH Q1A conditions: 25°C/60% RH, 30°C/65% RH, 40°C/75% RH, and 2–8°C cold storage).
