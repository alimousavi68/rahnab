### Challenge 1: The Tablet Portrait (768px–1024px) Value-Chain Cramping
- **Assumption Challenged:** The blueprint defines the horizontal value-chain pipeline for desktop and reflows to a vertical stepper at `< 768px`.
- **Attack Scenario:** On an iPad or tablet in portrait orientation ($768\text{px} - 834\text{px}$ width), displaying 5 horizontal pipeline stages results in column widths under $140\text{px}$. In Persian typography, compound words like «آزمایشگاه کنترل کیفی» or «پالایشگاه پلاسمای انسانی» will suffer severe line breaks, wrapping across 4–5 lines, or overflow out of bounds.
- **Blast Radius:** Broken layout and poor reading ergonomics on tablet portrait devices.
- **Mitigation for Milestone 3:** Enforce the vertical stepper reflow at `lg` breakpoint (`< 1024px`), reserving the horizontal 5-stage pipeline exclusively for viewports $\ge 1024\text{px}$.

### Challenge 2: Hybrid Dual-Accent Color Role Discipline
- **Assumption Challenged:** Utilizing Kinetic Amber (`#FD7702`) as holding primary and Clinical Emerald (`#00A896`) as a bio-ecosystem token.
- **Attack Scenario:** If developers or designers in Milestone 3 use Emerald for interactive buttons or links, the interface will feature competing colored CTAs. The user will be unsure which action is primary.
- **Blast Radius:** Visual noise, lack of CTA hierarchy, diluted conversion rates.
- **Mitigation for Milestone 3:** Enforce an ironclad component rule in `03_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` and `tokens.css`: Primary action buttons (`.btn-primary`) **ONLY** use Kinetic Amber (`#FD7702`). Clinical Emerald (`#00A896`) is strictly non-interactive or restricted to badges, status indicators, and SVG pipeline nodes.

### Challenge 3: Bilingual `<bdi>` Tag Automation in WordPress
- **Assumption Challenged:** Requiring `<bdi>` tags around all inline Latin acronyms.
- **Attack Scenario:** In production WordPress content creation, editorial personnel will paste Persian copy from Word or Google Docs and will not manually insert `<bdi>` or `<span dir="ltr">` around words like `CARTIMED`, `IVIG`, or `ISO 17025`.
- **Blast Radius:** Left-to-right punctuation clipping and misplaced parentheses in live articles.
- **Mitigation for Milestone 3/WP:** In the theme's `functions.php` / prototype JS engine, implement an automated text filtering utility that detects Latin alphanumeric sequences within Persian paragraphs and automatically wraps them in `<bdi>`.

### Challenge 4: Native `<dialog>` Modal Scroll-Bleed on iOS Safari
- **Assumption Challenged:** Relying on native HTML5 `<dialog>` for license and quick-reveal drawers.
- **Attack Scenario:** On iOS Safari, opening a native `<dialog>` does not prevent background body scrolling when touching outside modal bounds, leading to awkward dual scrolling.
- **Blast Radius:** Clunky mobile experience during accreditation audit.
- **Mitigation for Milestone 3:** In the prototype modal controller (`dialog.js`), attach an event listener to lock `document.body.style.overflow = 'hidden'` on `dialog.showModal()` and restore on `dialog.close()`.
